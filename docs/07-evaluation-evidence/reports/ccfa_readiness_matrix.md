## Current Blocking Items

- Board-native source-line/DWARF attribution remains limited to ELFs that actually carry DWARF. The debug/no-PIE toolchain path and 12 debug ELF rerun candidates are proven, but current generated board ELFs remain function-level with sidecar/source-equivalent line evidence, and no accepted board rerun proves exact captured-ELF hash linkage.
- Real malware validation is not claimed and remains out of scope. The current success condition is malware-like behavior audit over safe surrogate workloads.
- Genesys2 board benign-control false-positive evidence is accepted only for the 30 non-network controls in the artifact-backed external summary; it is not randomized benign workload coverage.
- Hardware pointer evidence includes bounded-prefix ARG_MEM plus the accepted v3 openat/write/execve full-string groups. It does not claim generalized all-pointer recovery, raw payload release, kernel memory capture, or full memory dumps.
- Companion-derived strings are trusted semantic companions only and are not hardware-derived strings.
- The active evidence package is selected by `current/latest_manifest.json`; dated board result directories are provenance, not a chronological latest-selection rule.
- Four current-bitstream P0 failed attempts are retained and not counted as PASS: `file_open_read_write/rep_09`, `fork_exec/rep_06`, `fork_exec/rep_07`, and `illegal_instruction/rep_02` have sequence gaps.
- The statistical robustness audit is limited to controlled repetitions and retained failures. It does not claim randomized workload generalization, real-malware generalization, or production long-run stability.
- Current accepted trace evidence is marker-window BRAM evidence, not a production streaming/DMA sink throughput claim. The trace-export decision, p50/p95/p99 target, and non-BRAM transport readiness gates are complete, but UART/DMA/Ethernet/PCIe streaming execution remains external/open.
- The artifact package is lightweight and manifest-based. It supports fresh-clone checker reproduction but does not bundle large raw board artifacts or prove an independent external rerun.
- `external_closure_readiness.json` fixes the acceptance standards for remaining non-real-malware external work, but it is not a substitute for the needed board/RTL evidence.
- `external_closure_intake.json` now validates optional future external summaries. Its current `BLOCKED_EXTERNAL_ARTIFACTS_REQUIRED` status means three external summaries are accepted, while production streaming/DMA remains invalid and open.
- `external_closure_plan.json` makes the remaining external work executable and reviewable, but its templates are not evidence and cannot substitute for board/RTL execution.
- `external_closure_preflight.json` proves the local command/schema guardrails are ready, but it is not external execution and cannot substitute for board/RTL evidence.
- External summary templates under `external_closure_templates/` are scaffolding only, not intake evidence; future accepted summaries must include artifact-backed `evidence_artifacts` rows with matching sha256 values.
